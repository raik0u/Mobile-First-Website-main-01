Latte Macchiato

Meaning "stained milk" in Italian, this elegant drink features three distinct layers. Smooth, hot milk rests at the bottom, a rich espresso shot creates the warm middle ring, and a generous cap of fluffy foam finishes the top. Milder than a latte, with a wonderful balance of creamy sweetness and bold coffee.
